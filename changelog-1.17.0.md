## What's new

### Home screen widgets
- Four new widgets bring your shopping list to the home screen: long-press the home screen → Widgets → HomeShopList.
  - **Shopping list:** the list with input field, category choice and list switch.
  - **List:** just the shopping list.
  - **Quick add:** just the input field with category choice.
  - **List switch:** shows the current list with the number of open items and switches it.
- Tap an item to tick it off, or back on. "Clear completed" below the completed items removes them after a confirmation, just like in the app.
- Items are grouped by category in the order of the list's store, like in the app.
- Tap the input field to add items: the input opens right over the widget, with suggestions from your product history. It stays open, so you can add several items in a row.
- The symbol next to the input field chooses the category for new items.
- Tap the list name to switch the list. The switch applies to the app and all widgets.
- The app logo and the arrow at the top right open the app.
- All widgets can be resized. The slim widgets (quick add, list switch) only show their bar, so they take up as little space as possible.
- Widgets follow the app's colours in light and dark mode.

### Help
- New help section about the widgets, in all 13 app languages.

### Fixes
- The app logo in the header is no longer blurry when the app starts while it is still connecting (e.g. when opened from a widget).
- Product suggestions now have the same height with and without the delete button.
