## What's new

### Wear OS tile
- New tile showing the number of open items with a progress ring and the app branding.
- Tap the tile to open the list on the watch.
- The tile preview in the tile picker shows the new design.

### Wear OS app
- New header with logo and list name.
- Changes made on the phone now show up on the watch within seconds.
- Larger touch targets and a better layout on round screens.
- During setup, **Open on phone** opens the app on the paired phone.
- Fixed a crash when opening the app.

### Settings
- New help for **List display**, **Backup and restore** and **Updates** (GitHub version only).
- Tab labels stay on one line; long translations use a slightly smaller font instead of wrapping.
- Italian: the **View** tab is now called **Vista**.
- English: "enable Notifications" is now "Enable notifications".

### Main screen
- The "New item" placeholder stays on one line, so the input field keeps its height on narrow screens and with large system fonts.

### Google Play
- Store listing in 29 languages with new descriptions, screenshots and feature graphic.
